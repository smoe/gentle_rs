## Contract

Enable native preparation, explicit completed FASTQ/gzip selection, reviewed
sample/checksum/policy bindings, nonmodal screening and monitoring, cooperative
cancellation, bounded evidence inspection and summary export. Reuse existing
gene sets, shared operations, parser, command service and scientific records.
No GUI-owned classifier, forced isoform winner, expression/molecule estimate,
implicit download/directory watcher, full decompression or upfront checksum pass.
Twenty targets are a functional fixture, not a throughput or specificity claim.

Execution lifecycle, scientific outcome and project attachment are separate.
Only an explicitly audited artifact-only operation can avoid a detached commit.
Keep normal mutation guards and stale-owner rejection. A completed publication
cannot be relabelled by a later service cancel. Engine cancellation before
publication remains valid. A control fault remains failed scientific analysis,
not authorized cancellation. Verified units stay inspectable in partial reports.

Bind submitted canonical requests, effective requests, reviewed panel hashes,
published report content and exact artifact locations. Manifest resolution may
normalize/fill MD5 fields; never equate raw submitted and effective bytes or
re-resolve a changed manifest to infer an old binding. Optional fields must be
omitted when absent to preserve legacy semantic hashes/cancel identities.
Do not infer a new result from an existing output filename.

  bounded. Writes and project replacement retain parsed-command approval.
